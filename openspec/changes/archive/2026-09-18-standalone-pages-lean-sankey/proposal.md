## Why

Each page is to stand alone at its own path — opened by its URL, never reached from another page — so the shell's cross-page navigation (the two-level `Category` / `View` control) is coupling with no consumer, and the Network category's Graph view, a cytoscape drawing of the same trace body the Network Sankey already explains with residuals and conservation, is a fourth route nobody needs to reach. On both Sankeys the title-bar row and the numeric summary below the chart take height from the diagram (the summary was already folded on arrival for that reason) while the scope bar is already a row of controls closed by Query. And the two Sankeys, built on one `sankey-canvas`, have drifted in look: the trace paints tooltip rows in the colour of the mark they describe, prints a card's attributes as lines, and marks every ribbon's direction with a chevron; the storage Sankey does none of these.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING** — **Three standalone pages.** `/graph` (Storage Graph), `/sankey` (Storage Sankey) and `/network/sankey` (Network Sankey) are the only routes. `/network/graph` is removed and answers with the not-found page; `/network` redirects to `/network/sankey` keeping the query; `/` still redirects to `/graph`. Each page keeps its own loader, scope, draft and URL contract exactly as today.
- **BREAKING** — **The nav bar loses its `Category` and `View` controls.** It keeps the application name, the demo badge, the view time range, the status indicator with Reload, and the theme control. No page links to another page from the shell; the not-found page's link to `/graph` stays.
- **The Network page is one Sankey page**, no longer `/network/:view`. Its Locate is gone with its target: no Network Sankey card is presented as clickable. The Storage Sankey's Locate into `/graph` stays as it is.
- **Dead Network-Graph code goes**: `GraphView`'s `unconfiguredMessage` prop and `describeGraphOutcome`'s `unconfigured` outcome (no page sets them once the Storage Graph is the only `GraphView`), the `:view` route guard, `keepSearch`, and the category helpers of `routes.ts`.
- **Both Sankeys lose their title-bar row and their numeric summary.** The row's controls move into the page's scope bar as a trailing **view-controls group** after the Query action: on the Storage Sankey `Mode` (`Read` / `Write` / `Both`), `Layout` (`Flat` / `Node`), `SVM` (`Column` / `Group` with its unavailable reason), the Top pods cut statement (`N of M pods`, beside the Top pods field) and the legend (status dots, read / write swatches); on the Network Sankey `Group`, `Order`, `Min Δ` with its readout and `Clear`, the `hidden …` pill and the presence-gated trace legend. `SankeySummary`, `TraceSummary` and the shared `SummaryPanel` are deleted; the trace's warnings drawer becomes a **warnings pill** in the same group (count, messages on hover / focus). Focus mode still hides the whole scope bar, so nothing changes about what it collapses.
- **The Storage Sankey follows the trace's look** in three places:
  1. **Tooltip rows are painted like their marks** — `in read` / `out read` (and a read link's value row) in the read ribbon colour, `in write` / `out write` (and a write link's) in the write colour; every other row stays plain, as the trace's do.
  2. **Cards print attribute lines** — the subtitle becomes the kind alone (`· <ontap_cluster>` for NetApp kinds, `· no flow` for a no-flow root), and `ns/<namespace>`, `usage <used> / <capacity> (<pct>%)`, `<n> pods` and, on a leaf, `total <inflow>` each take one monospace line, sizing the card as the trace's `hopHeaderH` does. The namespace stripe stays.
  3. **Every read / write ribbon ends in a direction chevron**, drawn by one primitive shared with the trace through `sankey-canvas`.

### Modified Capabilities

- `app-shell`: "View routing" (three routes, `/network` → `/network/sankey`, no `/network/graph`), "Top nav bar" (no category / view controls; the page-owned control bar now also holds each Sankey's view controls and legend), "Page-owned data lifecycle" and "Reload action and status indicator" (three pages, no view switch), "Page transient state lives and dies with the route" (the trace `Group` / `Order` and the summary's collapse are gone or moved); the requirement "The Network category is one page with one loader across its views" is removed.
- `storage-flow-sankey`: "Nodes are presented as box cards" (attribute lines, kind-only subtitle), "Links are gradient ribbons on a shared scale" (end chevron), "Labels and tooltips for nodes and links" (painted flow rows), "Layout switch: flat and node grouping", "SVM display switch: column and group", "Top pods projection" and "Weights come straight from the backend" (the controls and legend live in the scope bar's view-controls group; the cut statement moves beside Top pods), "Flow chain and tier structure" (derived marks in the tooltip only), "Focus mode" (no title bar or summary to collapse), "Fully independent of the Graph view's controls" (its scenarios reach `/graph` by Locate, not by a nav link); "Numeric summary outside the chart" is removed. "Clicking a node Locates across views" is unchanged.
- `network-trace`: "Input is the trace fetch, shared by both Network views" is replaced by "Input is the trace fetch" (one Network page, one view) and "No card offers Locate" is added; "Layout and order switches", "The `Min Δ` display threshold" (in the scope bar's view-controls group; the pill's text as the view prints it) and "Legend" (in the scope bar's view-controls group), "Zoom, keyboard and focus are the shared behaviour" (focus collapses the scope bar) are modified; "Empty states" is replaced by "Empty states by cause" (the warnings pill replaces the drawer, and an empty 200 body is the `trace-empty-response` answer the view has always drawn, not a model error); "Clicking a card Locates into the Network Graph" is removed.
- `sankey-canvas`: a new requirement for the shared **ribbon end chevron**, so both Sankeys draw it from one primitive. The view-controls group's placement is specified in `app-shell` ("Top nav bar") and each Sankey's own spec.
- `runtime-config`: the `endpoints.trace` requirement names one Network page and no nav bar; the `endpoints.storageGraph` requirement's "reachable via the nav bar" becomes "reachable at its path".
- `dev-environment`: the trace fixture's demo-mode scenario no longer clicks a Graph view link; the fixture coverage test keeps `host` / `network-flow` covered by the trace fixture's normalized elements.
- `graph-view`: the `host` / `network-flow` registration requirement keeps its rules; its scenario renders the trace fixture into the Graph view directly rather than through `/network/graph`.
